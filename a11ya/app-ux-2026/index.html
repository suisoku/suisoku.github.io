Recommended: Studio, with Ledger’s reporting discipline.

Keep one stable shell and progressive evidence detail. Use document structure for longer explanations; introduce Desk as a focused review mode only when a real review workflow exists.

Every concept includes overview, projects, history, audit setup and confirmation, live browser states, results, organization, account/settings and recovery examples. Switch EN/FR in the header. Forms and dialogs simulate interactions; no accounts, audits or invitations are created. No data persists and no remote assets are loaded.
